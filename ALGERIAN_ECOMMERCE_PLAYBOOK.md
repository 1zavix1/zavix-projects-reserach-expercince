## 1. Mindset & Core Principles

### The Gambling Trap
- E-commerce is **not** gambling. You do not "recover losses" by betting more.
- Professionals cut losing tests early and scale only what is proven.
- Every decision must be based on **data and predefined rules**, not emotion.

### The Research Trap
- Endless research without launching is the #1 killer of Algerian e-commerce beginners.
- Rule: **Research → Hypothesis → Test → Data → Decision.**
- Stop researching when you have enough to run a small test.

### The Silver Bullet Myth
- There is no perfect product. There are only **tested products**.
- You validate demand with small budgets before buying bulk.

### Core Principles
1. Start small. Test cheap. Scale only winners.
2. Cash-on-Delivery is a trust game — confirmation is everything.
3. If RTS (Return-to-Sender) is above 20%, fix confirmation before scaling.
4. Track every number in a spreadsheet. No tracking = no learning.
5. AI is a tool, not a replacement for judgment.

## 2. The Algerian Market Reality

### Consumer Behavior
- **COD is king.** 90%+ of Algerian e-commerce uses cash on delivery.
- Mobile-first: 85% Android, 15% iOS.
- Trust is low. You must confirm orders via WhatsApp or phone.
- Price sensitivity is high. Most COD products sell between **2,000–6,000 DZD**.

### Payment Infrastructure
- CCP (Algérie Poste) is the main payout method.
- International payments: Binance P2P → USDT → RedotPay/Virtual Cards.
- Meta/TikTok ads require foreign payment methods — use virtual cards.

### Delivery Landscape
- **Yalidine** and **Maystro** are the main couriers.
- Home delivery costs vary by wilaya: ~400–900 DZD.
- Stopdesk (pickup point) is cheaper but lower conversion.
- RTS rates are high if confirmation is weak — aim for <15%.

## 3. Legal Framework — Micro-Importation (Cabas)

The Algerian government legalized small-scale imports under **Micro-Importation**.

| Parameter | Regulation |
|-----------|------------|
| Legal Status | Auto-Entrepreneur Card via ANAE (Code 080000) |
| Frequency | Up to 2 trips per month |
| Import Cap per Trip | Up to 1.8 Million DZD |
| Customs Rate | ~5% simplified duty |
| Declaration | Mandatory via anae.dz portal |
| Banking | Foreign Currency Account (e.g., BEA) |
| Allowed Goods | Fashion, Cosmetics, Electronics, Gadgets |

**Note:** The parallel currency market adds 20–40% to your real COGS. Factor this into margins.

## 4. Product Research & Selection

### Product Criteria for Algeria COD
- Price: 2,000–6,000 DZD
- Weight: Under 500g
- Solves a real problem or has high "wow" factor
- Not easily found in local markets
- Easy to demonstrate in video
- Low return risk (avoid fragile electronics, consumables, size-specific items)

### Research Methods
1. **Facebook Ad Library** — See active ads targeting Algeria.
2. **TikTok Creative Center** — Top ads in MENA region.
3. **AliExpress/AliBaBa** — Search bestsellers, check order volume.
4. **Instagram Hashtags** — #fitnessdz #healthdz #gadgetdz
5. **Competitor pages** — Follow, engage, note their offers.

### The 3-Product Test
- Source 3 products at once.
- Test all 3 with the same ad budget.
- Scale the winner, kill the losers.

### Example First Product: Posture Corrector
- Low cost (~1,000–1,500 DZD landed)
- Light, easy to ship
- Solves real problem (back pain, bad posture)
- Easy to demo on video
- Can sell for 3,300–4,800 DZD

## 5. Sourcing — China, Turkey, UAE, Korea

| Country | Best For | Shipping Time | Unit Cost |
|---------|----------|---------------|----------|
| China | Electronics, gadgets, home tools | 25–45 days sea / 7–12 days air | Lowest |
| Turkey | Fashion, textiles, leather, beauty | 3–7 days air | Medium |
| UAE (Dubai) | Refurbished phones, fragrances | 2–4 days | Medium |
| South Korea | K-Beauty, skincare | 5–10 days | High |

### Platforms
- **Alibaba.com** — English, good for beginners.
- **1688.com** — Chinese domestic, 30–60% cheaper but needs agent.
- **AliExpress** — Good for samples and small orders.

### The Non-Negotiable Rules
1. Always order samples before bulk.
2. Do a video call with the supplier.
3. Use a third-party inspector (SGS) for bulk orders.
4. Calculate landed cost: Product + Shipping + Customs + Duties + 19% VAT.

## 6. Unit Economics & Financial Stress Testing

### The Core Formula

**Net Profit = Revenue − (COGS + Shipping + Call Center + Packaging + RTS Losses + Ad Spend)**

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Formula |
|--------|----------|
| Gross Profit | Revenue − COGS |
| Contribution Margin | Revenue − Variable Costs |
| Net Profit Margin | (Net Profit ÷ Revenue) × 100 |
| Break-Even CAC | Max Ad Spend ÷ Delivered Orders |
| Break-Even ROAS | Revenue ÷ Ad Spend at Break-Even |
| RTS Loss per Order | Outbound + Return + Penalty + Call + Packaging (+ COGS if damaged) |

### Stress Test Template (Per 100 Raw Leads)

**Inputs:**
- Retail Price (R) = ?
- COGS (C) = ?
- Shipping (S) = ?
- Call Center Fee = 150 DZD
- Packaging = 80 DZD
- Confirmation Rate = 60%
- RTS Rate = 25%
- Return Penalty = 250 DZD

**Waterfall:**
- Raw Leads: 100
- Confirmed: 60
- Delivered: 45
- RTS: 15

**Revenue:** 45 × R  
**Costs:** COGS + Shipping + Call + Packaging + RTS Penalties

**Break-Even CAC per Delivered Order** = (Revenue − Costs) ÷ 45

### Sensitivity Table (Example)

| RTS Rate | Delivered | Revenue | Op Costs | Net Profit | Margin |
|----------|-----------|---------|----------|------------|--------|
| 15% | 51 | 244,800 | 138,300 | 106,500 | 43.5% |
| 20% | 48 | 230,400 | 136,800 | 93,600 | 40.6% |
| 25% | 45 | 216,000 | 135,300 | 80,700 | 37.4% |
| 30% | 42 | 201,600 | 133,800 | 67,800 | 33.6% |

## 7. Store Setup — Shopify + COD Form

### Why Shopify
- Fastest way to launch a professional store.
- Built-in order management and pixel integration.
- Free COD form apps available.
- Cost: ~$1 first month, then $39/month.

### Setup Checklist

1. **Create account** → Select "Sell online" → Temporary store name.
2. **Settings → Payments** → Manual payment method → Cash on Delivery.
3. **Settings → Shipping** → Create zone: Algeria → Free shipping (or flat rate).
4. **Settings → Checkout** → Guest checkout, phone required.
5. **Add product** → Title, description, images, price in DZD.
6. **Install COD Form app** — Releasit or COD Form & Upsell.
7. **Configure form fields:** Name, Phone, Wilaya, Commune, Address.
8. **Button text:** "Confirmer la commande – Paiement à la livraison"
9. **Facebook Pixel** → Online Store → Preferences → Paste Pixel ID.
10. **Test order** → Place a fake order, check pixel fires.

### Avoid These Mistakes
- Don't buy a custom domain for the first test.
- Don't install more than 2–3 apps.
- Don't spend hours on design. Use the free Dawn theme.
- Don't enable multiple currencies.

## 8. Meta Ads — Full Technical Guide

### Campaign Structure (First Test)

- **Objective:** Sales (Conversions)
- **Budget:** CBO, 2,000–2,500 DZD/day
- **Audience:** Broad, Algeria, 18–55, no interests
- **Placements:** Automatic
- **Creatives:** 3 videos (Pain, Demo, Social Proof)

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Meaning | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| CPM | Cost per 1,000 impressions | 400–800 DZD |
| CTR | Click-through rate | 1–2% |
| CPC | Cost per click | 20–50 DZD |
| CVR | Conversion rate | 2–5% |
| CPA | Cost per acquisition | Below break-even |
| ROAS | Return on ad spend | Above 2.0 |

### Decision Rules

**Kill an ad:**
- CTR < 0.8% after 1,000 impressions
- CPM > 1,000 DZD for 3 days

**Kill the product:**
- No orders after 15,000 DZD ad spend
- CPA > 2,500 DZD after 25,000 DZD spend
- RTS > 40%

**Scale:**
- CPA < 1,600 DZD for 5+ days
- 10+ confirmed orders
- Increase budget 20–30% every 2–3 days

## 9. TikTok Ads — Full Technical Guide

### Campaign Structure

- **Objective:** Conversions (Complete Payment or Submit Form)
- **Targeting:** Smart+ or Broad, Algeria, 18–55
- **Creative:** Native UGC style, 9:16 vertical

- Look like a real review, not an ad
- Use Darija or French
- Show product in use
- Add text overlay: price + COD

## 10. COD Confirmation & Return-to-Sender Reduction

### The Confirmation Script

**French:**
> Bonjour [Name], nous avons bien reçu votre commande. Prix total: 3,300 DZD, livraison gratuite. Pour confirmer, répondez "OUI". Merci.

**Darija:**
> Salam [Name], rana reçu commande dyalk. Prix total: 3,300 DZD, livraison gratuite. Jawb b "OUI" pour confirmer. Merci.

### Rules
- Send WhatsApp within 5 minutes of order.
- Call if no reply in 1–2 hours.
- Do not ship unconfirmed orders.
- Track confirmation rate daily.

### Tools
- **Manual:** WhatsApp Business + Google Sheets
- **Automated:** Qunvert, Flashmanager, Releasit

## 11. Logistics, Delivery, & Cash Flow

### Delivery Flow
1. Order confirmed via WhatsApp
2. Pack product (polybag + thank-you note)
3. Drop off at Yalidine/Maystro
4. Courier delivers, collects cash
5. Courier transfers cash to your CCP account

### Cash Flow Reinvestment
- CCP Account ← Cash from COD
- Binance P2P ← CCP → USDT
- RedotPay Card ← USDT → Fund Meta/TikTok Ads

### Customs (for bulk imports)
- Use a local customs broker (transitaire)
- Clear goods within 21 days
- Pay Duties + 19% VAT on CIF value

## 13. Stop / Continue / Scale Rules

### Kill the Product
- No orders after 15,000 DZD ad spend
- CPA > 2,500 DZD after 25,000 DZD spend
- RTS > 40%
- Confirmation rate < 50%

### Continue Testing
- 3–5 confirmed orders after 15,000–20,000 DZD spend
- CPA between 1,800–2,500 DZD but decreasing
- Change one variable at a time (hook, price, headline)

### Scale
- CPA < 1,600 DZD for 5+ days
- 10+ confirmed orders
- RTS < 15%
- Increase budget 20–30% every 3 days

## 15. Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

|---------|----------|
